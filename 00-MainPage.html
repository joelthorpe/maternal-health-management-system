Maternal Health Management System

Supporting safer pregnancies and coordinated care

Our Mission

The specific problem that the system aims to address is the high maternal mortality rates across the region.

We have been reviewing data from organisations like WHO and UNICEF, and we have noticed that the problem isn't just a lack of medical facilities, but it is also a failure to effectively track and manage high-risk pregnancies across rural clinic locations.

Some areas may have regular check-ups for expectant mothers, whereas ones in remote villages may remain unmonitored until complications occur.

SDG Alignment

This system specifically aligns with the UN's Sustainable development goals, in specific number 3 (Good Health and Well-being).

My proposed system supports this in a meaningful way as it doesn't just look at statistics, it can actively help clinics manage the antenatal appointments and risk factors to prevent these maternal deaths happening in the first place.

System Features

This system acts as a web-based tracker where medical staff can log patient visits and flag risks related to their pregnancies.
